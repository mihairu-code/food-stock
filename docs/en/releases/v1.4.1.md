# v1.4.1 — Getting started on a full home

**November 2026**

## What's new for people

- **The getting-started window no longer slides off the screen.** On a long home the
  window pointed at an item far down, and on an iPhone the scroll to it didn't finish in
  time: the cutout and the window's buttons ended up at the very edge or below it, with no
  way out of the tour. Now the right spot lands in the middle of the screen at once — when
  a window opens and on every "Next": after "New section" at the bottom, the screen moves
  up to the section's pencil at the top by itself.
- **Getting started points at the first item at home,** not the newest: the one near the
  top, in the first expanded section. For a new person it's still the item they've just
  added.

## What changed in the code

- `CoachWindow`: an instant scroll to the element (`bringIntoView`) on open and in
  `goTo` — the page is released for a moment, scrolled and frozen again.
  `findCoachTarget` doesn't count elements inside `inert` as visible (rows of a collapsed
  section).
- `firstOnBoard` in `pages/home/model` — the first item of the first expanded section;
  the tour steps and the drag tip use it. Collapsed sections are a shared store
  (`useCollapsedIds`), the `home-stock:collapsed-sections` key is unchanged.
- `parseTourStep`: a step past "done" counts as done — if there are ever fewer steps, a
  finished tour won't start over.
