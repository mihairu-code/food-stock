# v0.8 — Installing the app

**November 15, 2026**

## What's new for people

- **"Browser or app".** On the welcome screen, under the sign-in buttons, there's a
  card: you can keep going in the browser, or put the app on the Home Screen or on the
  computer.
- **Steps for your device:** iPhone and iPad (Safari and other browsers), Android, a
  computer with Chrome or Edge, Safari on a Mac ("Add to Dock"). A browser that can't
  install is told honestly where to do it.
- **A scheme for every step:** a simplified drawing of the system UI with the button to
  press outlined. The schemes use the app's colors and theme.
- **An "Install app" button** in Chrome and Edge — the system install dialog with no
  instructions; the steps stay under "How to install manually".
- In the app opened from the Home Screen there's no card — nothing to offer.
- With "Reduce motion" on in the system, animations are off.

## What changed in the code

- `lib/install.ts` — what to offer based on the `userAgent` and two flags, with tests on
  real browser strings. `lib/installPrompt.ts` catches `beforeinstallprompt` at startup.
- `app/install/` — the card, the steps and the schemes (plain markup and lucide icons,
  no screenshots).
- Docs: [iPhone notes](../ios-notes.md) and `CONTRIBUTING`.
