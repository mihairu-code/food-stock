# v1.0 — One name, one thing

**November 20, 2026**

The first version I'm comfortable calling done: everything planned at the start works,
and over two weeks it gained editing, delete with undo, drag and drop, themes and icons
of your own, and installing on any device.

## What's new for people

- **One name, one thing.** A manual item named like a product that's at home isn't
  created — the product itself goes on the list. A new or renamed product takes the place
  of a manual item with the same name. Buying merges twins left by older versions — under
  one toast with "Undo".
- **Calmer drag and drop:** the place where the row or section will land is empty, without
  a half-transparent ghost; rows make way more smoothly, and a dropped one lands exactly
  at the end of its flight.
- Screen readers understand screens better: the header, settings groups and hints are
  marked up by meaning.

## What changed in the code

- Semantic markup, a shared `RowText` — [decision 0016](../decisions/0016-semantic-markup.md).
- Repetition extracted: `AuthScreen`, `useDragDraft`, `useNameWithIcon`, `SectionHeading`.
