# v0.1 — MVP

**November 8, 2026**

The first version you can actually use at home.

## What the app does

**Home.** Your own sections as collapsible cards, each with its products. A product has
a name, an emoji and a kind: *staple* or *one-off*. The mark on the right says whether
it's at home. A staple that runs out stays in its section, greyed out, and shows up in
shopping. A one-off only exists while it's at home, so marking it out just removes it.
The header shows how many products are at home.

**Sections.** Create with a name and an icon, rename, delete. A section with products
asks for confirmation first — its products go too, including those on the shopping list.

**Shopping.** One list: staples that ran out (with the section they belong to) plus items
added by hand. "Bought" on a staple puts it back home. "Bought" on a manual item asks
which section to put it into and whether to make it a staple.

**Deleting.** Swipe a row left. With a mouse or keyboard there's a button that shows up
on hover and focus.

**Settings.** Theme (system, light, dark). With Supabase: members, invite by email,
revoke an invite, remove a member, sign out. In demo mode: reset the sample data.

**Sign-in and homes.** A one-time code from an email, no passwords. On first sign-in
you either accept an invite or create your own home. Changes from other members arrive
live.

**PWA.** Installs to the home screen, the app shell is cached.

## Known limitations

- Sign-in by email code is awkward on a phone: switch to mail, copy, come back.
- Supabase's built-in mail only reaches members of your Supabase organisation.
- Every action waits for the server before the screen updates.
- You can't put something that's still at home on the shopping list.
- Removing a staple from the shopping list deletes the product itself.
