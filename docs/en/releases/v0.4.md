# v0.4 — Stock and shopping apart, four languages

**November 11, 2026**

## What the app does

Everything from v0.3, plus:

**Two independent marks.** At home every product has a dot on its icon: filled — at
home, empty ring — ran out. Tap the icon or the name to switch it. The "+ / −" button on
the right puts the product on the shopping list or takes it off, without touching stock.
So you can restock milk that's still there, or leave a product that ran out and not buy
it. A staple that runs out still goes to shopping by itself; a one-off can run out too
now and stays in its section.

**Shopping.** A product from home shows its section and whether it's "still at home" or
"ran out". "Bought" puts it back home. A swipe takes it off the list without buying —
the product stays in its section.

**Floating tabs.** No bottom bar: three separate buttons float over the content, the
shopping one shows how many items are on the list.

**Four languages.** Russian, English, Japanese, Vietnamese; "as on the device" by
default. Plural forms and dates follow the language. Emoji suggestions understand all
four: "strawberries", "いちご", "dâu tây".

**Sign out.** A Google account can sign out on this device; the homes stay in the
account. Google always asks which account to use.

## Known limitations

- Sheets with forms on iPhone sometimes hide under the keyboard.
- No way to reorder products or sections.
