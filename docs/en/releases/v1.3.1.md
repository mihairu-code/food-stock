# v1.3.1 — Before launch: leaving a home and a loading picture

**November 2026**

## What's new for people

- **"Leave home".** In the settings, in the Home group, with a confirmation: access to
  the home goes away and the only way back is a new link. If the owner leaves, the member
  who has been in the home the longest becomes the owner, and the confirmation names
  them. Another home opens next, or a new empty one if there is none. A lone member
  doesn't see the row, and demo mode doesn't have it.
- **"You were removed from “…”".** When the owner removed a member, the app used to open
  another home or create a new empty one without a word. Now it says what happened, even
  if the removal happened while the app was closed.
- **iPhone and iPad: sign in first, then add to the Home Screen.** In a browser without
  an account, once the home has items, the settings show "Your home is stored in Safari:
  the Home Screen app won’t see it" above "Sign in with Google". The installed app has
  its own storage, and without signing in it would open with an empty home.
- **A second section with the same name isn't created**, just like products: the field
  shakes and says "This section already exists". Changing only a section's icon is fine.
- Names are compared without telling "ё" from "е" apart.
- **Loading with a picture.** Instead of a "Loading…" line: a fridge door opens at
  launch, items fill the shelves on Home, and the list gets ticked off in Shopping. The
  picture appears after 300 ms, so a quick load doesn't flash. With the system "Reduce
  Motion" setting it stands still and gently fades in and out.

## What changed in the code

- Migration `0013_leave_household`: `public.leave_household(p_household_id)` on top of
  `private.leave_home`; it refuses the last member —
  [decision 0022](../decisions/0022-leave-home.md). Backend method `leaveHousehold`,
  `useLeaveHousehold` and `leftHouseholdSelf` in `entities/household`, `nextOwner` in
  `entities/member`, the `LeaveHomeRow` in the settings.
- `HouseholdGate` remembers the open home and, when it disappears from a fresh list not
  because of leaving it, shows the `toast.removedFromHome` toast (`removedHome`).
- `SectionSheet` rejects a duplicate with `findNamed` and `useFieldError`;
  `normalizeName` treats "ё" as "е".
- `StatusNote` takes `loading: 'fridge' | 'shelves' | 'list'`, has `role="status"` and a
  `LoadingPicture` (inline SVG, `aria-hidden`); `--loading-size` token. Load errors stay
  text only.
- `useCollapsedSections(sections)`: the write is in `try`, ids of deleted sections are
  dropped on save when there is one home (the list is shared by every home on the
  device). Reads and writes in `preferredHousehold`, `userName`, `inviteToken`,
  `useProfileSync` and clearing the merge ticket are in `try` too; `saveMergeTicket`
  throws on purpose.
- Rollout order: migration 0013 first, then the merge into `main`.
