# v1.4 — Onboarding and tips

**November 2026**

## What's new for people

- **Hints come in a spotlight window.** Instead of paragraphs at the bottom of the
  screen, a window over the app: everything is dimmed and the button in question shows
  through a cutout. Windows come one at a time, at the moment they're needed, and never
  repeat:
  - sections but no items — the "Add an item to “Fridge”" row;
  - the first item — two steps: tapping an item means "it ran out", the plus means
    "running low, buy it ahead";
  - two items — the row jiggles to the left: that's how "Edit" and "Delete" open;
  - four items — a row can be dragged, into another section too;
  - rows in Shopping — the "Bought" tick;
  - after the first purchase, for an owner alone in the home — "Better together" with an
    "Invite" button;
  - twenty items — the search magnifier.

  Tapping inside the cutout presses the button itself where that's the whole point:
  add, buy, open search. Hints show for new users for the first three days, or when
  turned on.
- **"First steps · 2 of 5"** in the hint windows: create a section, add an item, mark
  something as run out, buy something, invite the people you live with.
- **Ready-made sections on an empty home.** Fridge and Cupboard are ticked, Freezer,
  Treats and Vegetables are optional; "Create" makes all the ticked ones at once. "Your
  own section" adds your own button to them.
- **The first plus explains the orange dot:** "“Milk” is on the shopping list. It’s
  still at home, so the dot is orange". With "Not only colour" — "blue".
- **Tips.** The same window, but about habits: not before the third day and at most
  once a day. Buy one-off things straight from Shopping; remove what ran out long ago and
  isn't needed; sign in with Google so the home isn't lost (on an iPhone — before adding
  the app to the Home Screen); order the shopping list the way you walk through the
  shop; split a crowded section; turn on the minimal view; mark things as run out when
  you take the last one.
- **"Hints and tips" in the settings:** two switches and "Show hints and tips again".
- The "How to mark", "Smart list" and account paragraphs are gone: it's all in the
  windows now.

## What changed in the code

- The window lives in `shared/ui/coach`: `CoachHost` picks what to show and when,
  `CoachWindow` is a native `<dialog>` with a cutout, `useCoachPlacement` keeps the
  cutout in place through scrolling, rotation and font size changes. The queue, the marks
  and the pacing rules are in `shared/lib/coach/coach.ts`; the bubble's place is the pure
  function `placeCoach`. Tokens `--scrim` and `--spot-ring` —
  [decision 0023](../decisions/0023-spotlight-onboarding.md).
- A screen offers a window with `useCoach(id, request)`; elements are marked with
  `data-coach` (`ListRow`, `SwipeRow` and `AddRow` got a `coach` prop; the magnifier,
  the Shopping tab, section headers, the shopping list). Motion while a window is open is
  CSS on `data-coach-motion`.
- Home windows are in `useHomeCoach`, Shopping ones in `useShoppingCoach`, tips in
  `useHomeTips`; tip conditions are pure functions in `features/tips`. "First steps" —
  `shared/lib/coach/firstSteps.ts` and `useFirstStepsProgress` in `features/first-steps`.
- Reasons for tips (a recent delete, a reordered list, quick buys, the number of visits)
  are in `shared/lib/coach/tips.ts`, on the device.
- Ready-made sections — `ReadySections` in `pages/home`; `SectionSheet` got a pick mode
  (`SheetRequest` `add-section` with `pick`).
- New `localStorage` keys: `home-stock:coach-seen`, `home-stock:coach-snoozed`,
  `home-stock:tip-last`, `home-stock:first-steps`, `home-stock:tips`,
  `home-stock:tip-events`. No migrations.
- `Hint` lost the `onlyWhenHintsOn` flag; the `home.hint*`, `shopping.hint*`,
  `settings.anon*`, `settings.account{Label,Text}` and `home.createSection` keys are gone.
