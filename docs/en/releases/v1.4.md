# v1.4 — Getting started and tips

**November 2026**

## What's new for people

- **Getting started — five steps in a row.** Instead of paragraphs at the bottom of the
  screen, a window over the app: everything is dimmed and the button in question shows
  through a cutout. The window counts the steps — "Getting started · 3 of 5":
  1. "Add what you usually keep at home" — on the "Add an item to “Fridge”" row.
  2. One window, three cutouts: tapping an item means "it ran out", the plus means
     "running low, buy it ahead", the Shopping tab — "everything to buy ends up here".
  3. In Shopping, once something is there: the tick ("bought — it goes back home"), then
     "+ Add" for one-off things. While the list is empty, the tour waits.
  4. On Home the row jiggles to the left: that's how "Edit" and "Delete" open.
  5. "New section", then a section's pencil — rename or delete.

  A window closes only by its own button: a stray tap next to it won't dismiss it.
  Everyone goes through it, long-time users too — for them it starts at step 2.
- **Ready-made sections on an empty home.** Fridge and Cupboard are ticked, Freezer,
  Treats and Vegetables are optional; "Create" makes all the ticked ones at once. "Your
  own section" adds your own button to them.
- **Feature tips** after the tour, one at a time, at most once a minute: rearrange items
  (from three) and sections (from three), search by name (from twenty items), and right
  after the tour — invite the people you live with (it can also be done later in the
  settings).
- **Habit tips** from the third day, at most once a day: buy one-off things straight
  from Shopping; remove what ran out long ago; sign in with Google so the home isn't lost
  (on an iPhone — before adding the app to the Home Screen); order the shopping list the
  way you walk through the shop; split a crowded section; turn on the minimal view; mark
  things as run out when you take the last one.
- **The first plus explains the orange dot:** "“Milk” is on the shopping list. It’s
  still at home, so the dot is orange". With "Not only colour" — "blue".
- **"Getting started and tips" in the settings:** a "Tips" switch and "Go through getting
  started again".
- The "How to mark", "Smart list" and account paragraphs are gone.

## What changed in the code

- The window lives in `shared/ui/coach`: `CoachHost` picks what to show and when,
  `CoachWindow` is a native `<dialog>` with a cutout, `useCoachPlacement` keeps the
  cutout in place through scrolling, rotation and font size changes. The queue, the marks
  and the pacing are in `shared/lib/coach/coach.ts` (window kinds `tour`, `feature`,
  `tip`); the bubble's place is the pure function `placeCoach`. Tokens `--scrim` and
  `--spot-ring` — [decision 0023](../decisions/0023-spotlight-onboarding.md).
- The tour is `shared/lib/coach/tour.ts` (the step number on the device), its steps are in
  `useHomeCoach` and `useShoppingCoach`. Feature tips are in `useFeatureTips`, habit tips
  in `useHomeTips`; conditions are pure functions in `features/tips`, the reasons kept on
  the device are in `shared/lib/coach/tips.ts`.
- A screen offers a window with `useCoach(id, request)`; elements are marked with
  `data-coach` (`ListRow`, `SwipeRow`, `AddRow` and `SummaryBar` got a `coach` prop; the
  magnifier, the Shopping tab, a section's header and pencil, the shopping list). Motion
  while a window is open is CSS on `data-coach-motion`.
- Ready-made sections — `ReadySections` in `pages/home`; `SectionSheet` got a pick mode
  (`SheetRequest` `add-section` with `pick`).
- New `localStorage` keys: `home-stock:tour`, `home-stock:coach-seen`,
  `home-stock:coach-snoozed`, `home-stock:tip-last`, `home-stock:tips`,
  `home-stock:tip-events`. `home-stock:hints` is no longer read. No migrations.
- `Hint` lost the `onlyWhenHintsOn` flag; `shared/lib/hints` is gone; the `home.hint*`,
  `shopping.hint*`, `settings.anon*`, `settings.account{Label,Text}`, `settings.hints*`
  and `home.createSection` keys are gone.
