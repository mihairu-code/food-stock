# v0.3 — Google account

**November 10, 2026**

## What the app does

Everything from v0.2, plus:

**Welcome screen.** A new visitor chooses: sign in with Google or continue without an
account. While Google sign-in isn't configured on the server, there's no screen at all —
the app starts like v0.2.

**Google from settings.** Someone already using the app without an account can sign in
with Google in Settings → Account. Everything they've created moves into the account.

**One person, one account.** Signing in on a device that already has an anonymous user
merges that user into the account: homes move over, duplicates disappear, the anonymous
user is deleted. On the second device of the same person the duplicate member simply
goes away.

**Choosing a home.** If both the device and the account have homes with data, the app
asks which to keep and spells out what happens to the other one: deleted with its data,
or — if other people live there — you just leave it.

**Who's who.** Members show the account email, or, without an account, the device they
last used: "Safari · iPhone", "Приложение · iPhone".

**Invites survive Google.** Open an invite link, sign in with Google — and you still
join that home.

## Known limitations

- No sign-out yet.
- Removing a staple from shopping still deletes the product.
- Russian only.
