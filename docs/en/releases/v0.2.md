# v0.2 — No sign-up

**November 10, 2026**

## What the app does

Everything from v0.1, plus:

**No sign-up.** On first launch a user and a home called "Our home" are created
silently. No email, no codes, no sign-in screen.

**Invite links.** The owner taps "Invite by link" in settings and gets a link to share
or copy. It works once and lives 7 days; it's shown only at creation. Unused links are
listed with their expiry date and can be revoked.

**Joining.** Open the link and you're in the home. If the app is already on your home
screen, paste the link inside it: Settings → "Join by link". The empty home created on
first launch disappears after you join someone else's.

**Members.** An optional name visible to the other members. The owner can remove anyone
but themselves.

**Emoji by name.** Icon suggestions follow the product name as you type, in two rows,
nothing sticking out of the screen. "молоко 3,2%" gives 🥛 and other dairy, "сухарики" —
🥨 🍟 🥜 🍿. Forgives a typo. A confident match fills itself in; a manual choice wins.

**Instant edits.** Sections, products and shopping items show up right away; the request
goes after. On an error the change rolls back and a toast explains why. Short toasts
confirm actions.

**Transitions.** Screens fade in when switching tabs, sections collapse smoothly.

**Deployed** on Vercel. Automated browsers (link previews, crawlers, e2e tests) see the
demo instead of creating real users; the site is hidden from search.

## Known limitations

- The user lives on one device: clear site data or switch phones and the home is gone
  for that person (unless invited again).
- Safari and the home screen app on iPhone don't share storage.
- Removing a staple from shopping still deletes the product.
- Russian only.
