# v1.4.2 — The drag tip comes later

**November 2026**

## What's new for people

- **The "Press and hold a row, then drag it" tip comes from seven items,** not three.
  With three items it appeared almost right after the tour, when there was nothing to
  rearrange yet; by seven the order inside sections starts to matter.

## What changed in the code

- `DRAG_PRODUCTS_AT` in `useFeatureTips` is 7. The number is updated in
  [decision 0023](../decisions/0023-spotlight-onboarding.md).
