# v0.10 — Undo for deletes, the sheet in the Home Screen app

**November 18, 2026**

## What's new for people

- **"Undo" after deleting.** A deleted product or shopping line disappears right away,
  and a toast offers to bring it back for 5 seconds — to the same place. Same for
  "remove from the list" and for buying a home product.
- **A long swipe deletes.** Pull the row past the middle — the delete button grows to the
  whole strip, let go — deleted. On Android there's a light vibration at that moment.
  Closing, the row springs back softly.
- Swipe buttons are rounded, and there are no lines between products anymore.
- **On desktop** the pencil and the cross appear to the left of "+", not in its place —
  no more deleting a product just by moving the mouse down the column of pluses. In the
  shopping list "Bought" is now a check button.
- **A sheet can be pulled down by its text field** — with a clear move down; sideways
  moves and holding stay with the field.
- **The sheet above the keyboard in the Home Screen app** no longer hides under it.
- Section tiles don't shift when picked, and no focus ring sticks to the first tile.
- Anonymous users who haven't opened the app for over a year are deleted automatically;
  Google accounts never are.

## What changed in the code

- `removeWithUndo` — a delayed request and "Undo" in the toast —
  [decision 0014](../decisions/0014-delete-with-undo.md).
- `lib/keyboard.ts`: the screen height is the probe's height, not its bottom; a
  self-check of the sheet's position as a fallback. The numbers from a screen recording
  are in the tests.
- Migrations `0010` (last visit, `mark_visit`) and `0011` (daily cleanup via `pg_cron`) —
  [decision 0015](../decisions/0015-forget-inactive-anonymous.md).
