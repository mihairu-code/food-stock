# v0.7 — Your own look and calmer sheets

**November 15, 2026**

## What's new for people

- **Accent color.** Next to the theme in settings — six colors: green, teal, blue,
  violet, rose, graphite. Each has its own shade for light and dark. The screen no
  longer flashes light on launch in the dark theme.
- **A home with an avatar.** The owner can rename the home and pick an emoji for it —
  it goes into the header instead of the initials. With several homes, the switcher is
  right there.
- **Member photos.** People who signed in with Google get their account photo in the
  member list.
- **Sections get their own screen** in settings: the list, product counts, rename and
  delete.
- **The section is picked with tiles**, not a native select: the keyboard doesn't hide,
  the sheet doesn't jump.
- **Our own icon panel.** The "＋" button in front of the suggestions opens ~160 emoji:
  food, drinks, household stuff, places.
- **"Don't add home"** — a manual purchase can be marked as bought without turning it
  into a home product.
- **Hints for the first days.** A new person sees how things work on Home and
  Shopping; after three days the hints go away, and settings can turn them back on or
  off right away.
- **Lighter delete swipe:** a quick flick works, the row slides out and collapses
  smoothly.
- Buttons react to presses, on iPhone too. Wide rows don't shrink, they dim — and only
  the ones that are pressable as a whole.
- **The sheet above the keyboard** now sits right in the app from the Home Screen too;
  the form tightens up so the buttons stay visible. Tapping a field no longer closes
  the sheet by accident, and the "Always keep at home" checkbox doesn't hide the
  keyboard.
- A new product is one-off by default.

## What changed in the code

- `lib/appearance.ts` — theme and accent; an inline script in `index.html` sets them
  before the first paint.
- Migration `0008`: `households.icon`, `household_members.avatar_url`. `sync_profile`
  takes the photo from the token and accepts only `googleusercontent.com` URLs.
- `FormSheet` and `NameIconFields` — a shared frame for every form in a sheet.
- `SectionPicker` instead of `<select>` — [decision 0009](../decisions/0009-section-tiles-not-select.md).
- `lib/keyboard.ts`: the sheet's position is a pure function of the current
  `visualViewport`, re-measured on a timer while the person types. The attempt with a
  remembered keyboard height was reverted —
  [decision 0010](../decisions/0010-sheet-from-current-viewport.md).
