# v1.1 — Still home, but on the list

**November 2026**

## What's new for people

- **An orange dot.** A product that's still at home but already on the shopping list gets
  an orange dot and the caption "Still home · on the list". The same dot shows on shopping
  list rows: in the shop you can see what you must buy (red) and what's optional (orange).
  Buy it — the dot goes away.
- **Not by colour alone.** A new setting under App: stock dots change shape (a half, a
  circle with a bar) and switch to safe colours — for people who find red and green hard
  to tell apart. Samples of the three dots sit next to it.
- Screen readers name the new state in words: "at home, on the shopping list".

## What changed in the code

- The dot state is `stockState`, with a test; markup uses `data-stock` instead of
  `data-missing` — [decision 0018](../decisions/0018-stock-dot-not-only-color.md).
- Tokens `--stock-low`, `--stock-low-safe`, `--stock-out-safe`; `--stock-in` is declared
  explicitly in the light theme.
- `colorSafe` in `appearance.ts` and in the `index.html` script, a new script hash in
  `vercel.json`.
- A `StockDotSamples` component; the `SettingToggle` caption accepts markup.
