# v1.3 — Simpler: no one-off products, the icon at install time

**November 2026**

## What's new for people

- **Any product that runs out goes on the shopping list by itself.** No more deciding
  whether it's a staple or a one-off: the "Always keep at home" checkbox in the product
  sheet and "Make it a staple" in the purchase sheet are gone. Back home — off the list.
- Minus works as before: it takes the product off the list, and it stays "ran out".
- All former one-offs are regular products now. The "Always at home" and "One-off" row
  captions are replaced by the state: "At home", "On the list · still home" (was "Still
  home · on the list" — the difference from "At home" now comes first), "Ran out" and
  "Ran out · on the shopping list".
- **Minimal view** — a setting, off by default. No captions on Home or in the shopping
  list, names centred in the row, and shorter rows: 52 instead of 60. The dot, the grey
  icon of what ran out and the minus button show the state; a manual item on the list is
  the one without a dot.
- A one-time thing can skip the home ("Don’t add to home" when buying a manual item)
  or be deleted once it's no longer needed.
- Buying a manual item now just says "Added to «Section»".
- **The icon is chosen before installing.** The icon tiles are now in the "Browser or
  app" card on the first screen. The settings keep the picker only while the app is
  open in a browser. The installed app has none: the icon can't be changed there
  anyway. The "Copy app link" button is gone.
- **The stock dot position is a setting.** On the icon, right (as before), on the icon,
  left, or after the name. It applies on Home and in the shopping list, together with
  "Not by colour alone".
- The Home hint and the empty shopping list say that whatever runs out goes on the list
  by itself.

## What changed in the code

- `withAvailability(available)` ignores the product kind: "ran out" always means
  `to_buy = true` — [decision 0020](../decisions/0020-no-one-off-products.md).
- `StapleCheckbox` is gone. `NewProduct`, `ProductPatch` and `purchaseShoppingItem` no
  longer take a kind; a new product is written with `NEW_PRODUCT_KIND = 'staple'`, an
  edit leaves `kind` alone, the RPC gets `p_make_staple = true`. The trigger is
  unchanged. Migration `0012` turns `one_off` into `staple`; demo data is converted
  when read.
- The `toast.purchasedStaple` and `toast.purchasedOnce` toasts are replaced by
  `toast.addedTo`; the `product.keep`, `product.keepHint`, `toast.out`, `product.staple`
  and `product.oneOff` keys are gone.
- `data-minimal` on `<html>` (`setMinimal`, key `home-stock:minimal`) hides `.row-meta`
  in list rows; the row height is the `--row-minimal-height` token.
- A row with `stock` has a second dot, `.row-name-dot`, after the name; `data-dot-place` on
  `<html>` decides which one shows (`setDotPlace` in `appearance.ts`, key
  `home-stock:dot-place`).
- Demo set: three sections of 8 products with all four states, so Home shows the search
  button.
- A new slice, `features/choose-app-icon` (`AppIconTiles`), for the install card and the
  settings — [decision 0021](../decisions/0021-app-icon-at-install.md). New key
  `install.chooseIcon`, new `settings.appIconMeta` text, `settings.copyAppLink` removed.
- `isStandalone()` in `shared/lib/device.ts` — one "installed" check instead of two.
- Flow tests rewritten for a single behaviour; the test for minus on a product that ran
  out stays.
