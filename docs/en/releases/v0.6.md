# v0.6 — Drag and drop, a sheet above the keyboard

**November 13, 2026**

## What's new for people

- **Your own order.** A product can be dragged up or down within a section, moved to
  another section, and the sections themselves can be reordered. On a phone hold the row
  for a moment, with a mouse just drag. From the keyboard: space to pick up, arrows to
  move, space to drop.
- **The shopping list can be reordered too** — say, in the order you walk through the
  store. A product put back on the list goes to the end.
- On Android dragging gives a light vibration. On iPhone vibration isn't available to
  web apps.
- **New look:** dotted background, translucent buttons. The header shows the home name,
  the number of members and their initials; the initials open the settings. Emoji
  suggestions fit in one row. The page no longer zooms on pinch or double tap.
- **Sheet above the keyboard.** On iPhone the keyboard covered the input in a sheet —
  now the sheet sits above it.
- A sheet can be swiped down; while it's open the page underneath doesn't scroll.

## What changed in the code

- dnd-kit for dragging, the order is stored in the database: migrations `0006`
  (`products.sort_order`, RPCs `reorder_products` / `reorder_sections`) and `0007`
  (`products.shopping_order`, `shopping_items.sort_order`). An empty place means "never
  reordered". See [decision 0008](../decisions/0008-drag-and-drop-dnd-kit.md).
- Reordering doesn't touch `updated_at`.
- `lib/keyboard.ts` writes how much of the screen the keyboard covers into CSS variables.
- `lib/scrollLock.ts`, `useSheetDismiss` — scroll lock and the swipe-to-close gesture.
- A dropped row no longer flashes at its old place: the draft order stays until fresh
  data arrives.

## Known issues

- On iPhone, in the app opened from the Home Screen, the sheet above the keyboard is
  sometimes positioned off — looking into it.
